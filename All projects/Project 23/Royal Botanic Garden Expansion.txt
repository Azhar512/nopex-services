Location: Riyadh, Saudi Arabia

The Royal Botanic Garden Expansion is a large-scale landscape development focused on creating a sustainable and visually engaging public environment. The BIM scope included landscape architecture, site grading, hardscape design, irrigation systems, planting layouts, pedestrian pathways, water features, site lighting, and utility coordination. Using BIM workflows, the project team developed an integrated digital model to coordinate landscape, civil, and utility systems, perform clash detection, optimize site layouts, and support construction documentation. The coordinated BIM model improved design accuracy, reduced construction conflicts, and enhanced project efficiency while supporting long-term facility management and maintenance. The project was developed to LOD 350–400, with LOD 500 as-built documentation for asset management.
